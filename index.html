Billing and provisioning for small hosting businesses

Features

Everything a small hosting business runs on

Made for one person or a small team selling hosting, domains and SSL. Every integration is a plugin you can switch off, and nothing you don't use gets in the way.

Billing on autopilot

  • Payment notices made and emailed ahead of each due date, with reminders
  • Card payments with Stripe, bank transfer, client credit
  • Optional invoices, VAT rules, EU reverse charge, several currencies
  • Quotes, coupons, price changes with notice

Hosting accounts, set up for you

  • Create, suspend, unsuspend and terminate on cPanel, Plesk, HestiaCP, DirectAdmin, CyberPanel and Enhance
  • VPS on Proxmox VE
  • Suspends after a grace period, unsuspends once paid
  • Compare your servers with your billing and fix the differences

Domains and SSL

  • Register, transfer and renew at your registrar, expiry dates kept in sync
  • Domain search for visitors and clients, TLD prices and margins
  • Nameservers, EPP codes, contacts and registrar lock
  • SSL certificates sold, validated and renewed

A client area clients like

  • Order with a cart, pay, see services and usage
  • Change hosting passwords, open cPanel or Plesk in one click
  • Public order pages, compare tables and a home page with your logo
  • Ten languages, two-factor sign-in, Google sign-in

Support built in

  • Tickets with departments, notes and canned replies
  • Email to ticket from your support mailbox
  • Knowledge base in every language
  • Announcements and messages to chosen clients

Servers you can see

  • Uptime from Uptime Kuma, UptimeRobot, HetrixTools or Better Stack
  • Daily DNS and HTTPS checks of every site, IP blacklist checks
  • Disk and bandwidth usage, with warnings before clients run out
  • Server status page for your clients

Reports and sales

  • Income by month, by product, by server, and a forecast
  • Sales opportunities: domains to transfer, domains without hosting
  • Leads with follow-up days, and a payment record per client
  • Every report exports to CSV

Open to your own tools

  • REST API with per-area keys and OpenAPI docs
  • Signed webhooks for orders, payments and more
  • Slack, Discord and Telegram notifications
  • Plugins from a signed catalogue, or your own

AI helpers, your choice

  • Drafts ticket replies, improves or translates what you write
  • Ask your reports a question in plain words
  • Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Kimi or a local Ollama
  • Off until you add your own key

Why GaiaCP

Yours to run, yours to keep

No licence fee

Free and GPL-3.0. No price per client or per service, now or when you grow.

Runs on your hosting

PHP and MySQL with a cron job. No SSH, Composer or Node needed, and it works in a subfolder.

Safe to re-run

Every automatic step checks before it acts and is logged, so nothing is charged, suspended or emailed twice.

Signed updates

One button updates GaiaCP after a backup, and only releases signed with the project's key are accepted.

Install

Running in ten minutes

You need PHP 8.3 or later, a MySQL or MariaDB database and a cron job.

  1. Download and unzip

    Get the latest release and unzip it on your hosting, at the site's root or in a subfolder.

  2. Open the installer

    Create an empty database and open the site. The installer checks your server, asks for the database and creates your admin account.

  3. Add the cron line

    Add the line it shows you, log in at /admin and follow the setup guide on the dashboard.

Coming from WHMCS? The import brings over your servers, products, clients, hosting accounts with their passwords, domains and unpaid invoices, with a dry run first. More in the installation guide.

GaiaCP is in alpha. Features, settings and the database may still change between versions, and some integrations haven't been tried against real servers yet. Keep backups (built in) and read each release's notes before updating. Bug reports and ideas are very welcome on GitHub.
